1. Empower (formerly Personal Capital) — Best Overall Free Financial Dashboard

Empower is the most complete free financial planning tool available in 2026. Connect all your financial accounts — checking, savings, investments, loans, real estate — and get a real-time dashboard of your entire financial life.

What it does for free:

  • Net worth tracking updated daily
  • Investment portfolio analysis with fee detection
  • Retirement planner that shows if you are on track
  • Cash flow analysis
  • Investment checkup comparing your portfolio to recommended allocation

The retirement planner alone is worth it. Enter your current savings, expected contributions, and retirement date. Empower runs thousands of simulations and tells you the probability of your money lasting through retirement. This analysis costs hundreds of dollars from a financial planner.

The catch: Empower will try to sell you their wealth management service. The calls can be persistent. Use the free tools and politely decline their services.

Best for: Anyone who wants a complete financial picture in one place.

3. Boldin (formerly NewRetirement) — Best Retirement Planning Tool

Boldin is specifically designed for retirement planning and goes deeper than any other free tool in this category.

What it does:

  • Detailed Social Security optimization — shows the best age to claim based on your situation
  • Roth conversion analysis
  • Healthcare cost planning in retirement
  • Estate planning integration
  • Monte Carlo simulations for retirement income

Why it stands out: Most free tools give you a simple retirement number. Boldin models the complexity of actual retirement — variable spending, healthcare costs, Social Security optimization, tax efficiency. It is genuinely sophisticated.

Price: Free tier covers most needs. PlannerPlus at $20/month adds advisor-level features.

Best for: Anyone within 10-20 years of retirement who wants serious planning.

5. Vanguard Retirement Income Calculator — Best Simple Retirement Check

If you want a quick, reliable retirement readiness check without complexity, Vanguard's free calculator is the most trusted simple tool available.

What it does:

  • Enter current age, retirement age, current savings, monthly contribution
  • Get a probability score for retirement readiness
  • See recommendations to improve your outcome

Why it stands out: Backed by Vanguard's research and updated with current market assumptions. No account required, no data saved, completely anonymous.

Best for: A quick retirement check without committing to a full planning tool.

6. I-ORP — Best Advanced Tax and Withdrawal Planning

I-ORP (Optimal Retirement Planner) is a free tool used by serious do-it-yourself investors for optimizing Roth conversions, Social Security timing, and withdrawal sequencing in retirement.

What it does:

  • Optimizes which accounts to withdraw from first in retirement
  • Models Roth conversion strategies
  • Minimizes lifetime tax burden across retirement
  • Social Security claiming optimization

Why it stands out: The tax optimization alone can save retirees tens of thousands of dollars. Professional financial planners charge $500-$2,000 for this analysis.

Best for: DIY investors approaching or in retirement who want to minimize taxes.

How to Use These Tools Together

You do not need all six. Here is a simple setup based on your situation:

If you are in your 20s-30s building wealth: Empower for net worth tracking + ProjectionLab for long-term modeling

If you are in your 40s-50s approaching retirement: Empower + Boldin for serious retirement planning

If you are married or in a partnership: Monarch Money for shared financial management

If you want maximum tax efficiency in retirement: Boldin + I-ORP together

Best Free Retirement Planning Software in 2026

Of the six tools reviewed above, four are specifically optimized for retirement planning. Side-by-side for the most-searched use case in 2026:

  • Boldin (formerly NewRetirement) — the most comprehensive free retirement modeling: Social Security optimization, Roth conversion ladders, Monte Carlo simulation across 1,000 scenarios. Free tier covers 90% of use cases; paid tier ($120/yr) adds tax-optimal withdrawal sequencing.
  • Empower (formerly Personal Capital) — best for tracking actual net worth alongside the projection. Free retirement planner pulls live account balances and projects to age 95 using historical market returns.
  • Vanguard Retirement Income Calculator — best free tool for a sanity check in under 5 minutes. Single-screen output: "you can safely spend $X/month for 30 years." Strictly simpler than Boldin or Empower.
  • ProjectionLab — best for users who want to model "what if I retire at 52 vs 55 vs 60" with branching scenarios side-by-side. Free tier supports the core engine; visual outputs are best-in-class.
  • I-ORP — free, ugly UI, but the only public tool that mathematically optimizes the order of withdrawals across taxable/traditional/Roth to minimize lifetime tax. For advanced users only.

For most people in 2026 the right stack is: Boldin for the deep plan once a year, Empower for the monthly tracking, and Vanguard's calculator as the quick sanity check between formal reviews.
